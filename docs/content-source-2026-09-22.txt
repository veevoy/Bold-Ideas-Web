CASE STUDY — MAPWHIZZ

Rescuing a stalled launch

Mapwhizz is a location-intelligence and travel-time analysis platform designed for businesses and property professionals. The client had a great business proposition but was trapped in development hell; they'd picked a partner who couldn't deliver a working product. The project was heavily overpaid (several times the original budget), communication had broken down, and both the backend and frontend codebases were a mess. Stuck with an unreleased, broken system, they needed a partner to cut through the chaos and salvage a path to market without an immediate, budget-destroying total overhaul.

The audit and the call

We audited both sides of the application to see what was salvageable. Instead of a massive ground-up rewrite that would kill the budget and delay launch indefinitely, we recommended patching and stabilising the critical backend core just enough to go to market, while completely replacing the broken user layer.

Live, and building for scale

The product is now live, stable, and serving users. Because stabilisation got the business moving first, the client avoided a huge upfront money pit. With it in production, we've moved into the planned next phase: building a robust new backend from scratch to replace the legacy mess and handle long-term scale.

THE OUTCOME

By engineering a new frontend first and phasing the backend rebuild into the scale stage, we provided a clear way out of the trap. We saved their launch, protected their budget, and set them up for high-growth scale.

01
What is Bold Ideas Consulting?

Bold Ideas Consulting is your senior product, tech, and AI consulting partner. Your go-to when you are desperate to get your bold ideas into the world. As an extension of your team, we provide honest strategy, plans, and implementation where your product will become strong and scalable.

02
What is a CTO (Chief Technical Officer)?

A CTO leads your technical strategy and development. The role ensures that the technical execution is aligned with your business goals and budgets. Many companies don't want to hire a full-time CTO and therefore choose to work with fractional CTOs or tech consultants (like us!).

03
Why responsible AI?

AI is basically used for everything right now, and many businesses see the advantages of AI in their day-to-day business and development. But if you don't take a step back and look at your choices or AI routes, it comes with big risks.

Many AI solutions out there are not built for sturdy and trusted products. They look fancy, but aren't secure, scalable, or easy to use or manage. When we work with AI, we only choose the tools and solutions that make sense for the build but also for scale.

04
Why do we work with non-profits and charities?

We want to be on the side that does good things. To be the technical guidance for those with amazing and kind ideas that help people. To be proud of what we do.

05
What AI tools do businesses use to develop products?

Some of the AI tools used to develop are Lovable, Claude, GitHub Copilot, and Replit. Many of our clients come to us after trying to develop their products with these tools.

06
What is the difference between vibe coding a product and a product ready for production?

Vibe coding is great for rapid prototyping and internal tools with limited risk. For production, though, it's not enough to just generate the code — it needs to go through review, testing, and a security audit. What matters isn't how the code was created, but whether it went through this process before being deployed with live data into a live environment.

And the downside: with a vibe-coded app the creator only sees what the user sees and what the AI presents to them. That can be fatal when it comes to hidden logic, data security, and stability. What the AI presents as "Done" might work on the surface, but the whole thing might be built on sand — and it can all come crashing down like a house of cards later.
