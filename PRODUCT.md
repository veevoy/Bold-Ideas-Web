# Bold Ideas Consulting

A public website for a product and AI consultancy working with purpose-led organisations. Its main task is to help visitors recognise their technical needs, understand the available support, see relevant evidence and book a 30-minute introductory conversation.

The current implementation includes the homepage, a case-study index, four standalone case studies using one data-driven template, a service-package index, and a testimonial page. Six services are grouped under four needs and share their content between the homepage and service index. Booking uses an external Google calendar link; email is available as an alternative. Services and contact also remain homepage sections. There is no CMS, database, authentication or form submission backend.

Preserve the supplied identity, British English copy and qualified GBP pricing. The current composition combines photographic hero/footer films with two illustrative scroll stories. Motion is optional; all substantive content remains available in static layouts.

Love Peace Harmony is described as product definition, MVP specification, controlled prototype and a roadmap. Kinable has a specification, working prototype and roadmap, with development under way towards commercial launch. Mapwhizz is described as stabilisation and a new frontend, with the remaining legacy-backend rebuild begun but not complete. Do not expand these into unverified quantitative outcomes or pretend illustrations are product screenshots. Corporate names in the experience strip refer to previous agency roles.
